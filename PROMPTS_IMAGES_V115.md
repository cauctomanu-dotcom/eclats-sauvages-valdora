## `game/assets/v115/legend_elemental_atlas_chibi3d_v115.png`

> Create a professional 2x2 sprite atlas for a modern chibi 3D top-down adventure RPG. Transparent background, no text, no labels, no border, no UI, no characters, no creatures. Four equal quadrants, each containing one coherent environmental prop cluster seen from a high three-quarter isometric/top-down game camera, soft PBR materials, rounded stylized proportions, strong readable silhouettes, polished console-game quality, consistent lighting and scale, generous transparent padding so nothing crosses quadrant boundaries.
> TOP LEFT — PRIMORDIAL FOREST: monumental ancient tree roots forming a natural arch, thick emerald vines, oversized fern, mossy standing stone, tiny warm firefly lights.
> TOP RIGHT — EMBER CALDERA: cracked black basalt, glowing lava fissure, obsidian spires, scorched trunk, small volcanic vent; rich orange internal glow.
> BOTTOM LEFT — TIDAL ISLAND: pale coral rock, turquoise tide pool, sea grass, shells, driftwood, foamy wet stones; tropical ocean colors.
> BOTTOM RIGHT — STORM PLATEAU: wet dark basalt slabs, copper lightning rod, scorched ground impact mark, wind-bent silver grass, charged blue crystals. Absolutely no clouds, no lightning-bolt icon, no painted lightning symbol; this is physical terrain dressing only because lightning will be animated in-engine.
> Use an orthographic chibi 3D aesthetic matching a polished creature-collection RPG. Transparent PNG with clean alpha edges.
